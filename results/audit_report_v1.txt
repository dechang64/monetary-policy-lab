   sentiment, NOT path shock (forward guidance). The "forward guidance

   - Sentiment responds to monetary policy shocks (H1: target significant)
   - Asset returns respond to target shocks (H2: significant for equities/gold)
   - Sentiment provides incremental information beyond rate changes
     (R² jumps from 0.4% with rate_change to 1.6% with GSS shocks)
   - But the "information channel via forward guidance" story is dead

   dominates on average), std is 0.005 not 0.032
